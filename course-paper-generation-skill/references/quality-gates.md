# Quality Gates

Before final delivery, check:

- The paper answers the assignment requirement.
- Title, abstract, headings, and conclusion use consistent terminology.
- Every major claim has evidence.
- Figures and tables are numbered in order.
- Captions, axis labels, and units are readable.
- Formulas include definitions for variables.
- Citations are present and not fabricated.
- DOCX opens correctly and remains editable.
- PDF export matches the DOCX layout.
- File names clearly indicate version and deliverable type.
