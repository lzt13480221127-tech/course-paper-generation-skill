# Academic Writing

## Style

- Use precise nouns and verbs.
- Make claims proportional to evidence.
- Replace task phrasing such as “本作业要求” with paper phrasing such as “本文围绕...展开分析”.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives.
- Keep one paragraph focused on one claim.

## Section Expectations

- Abstract: problem, method, result, implication.
- Introduction: background, gap or problem, purpose, contribution, structure.
- Method: assumptions, variables, formulas, procedure, parameters.
- Result: present data first, then interpret.
- Conclusion: answer the research question; include limitations when appropriate.

## Citations

If the user supplies sources, preserve source identity and citation order. If sources are missing, do not invent bibliographic details. Use explicit placeholders such as `[待补充文献1]`.
